Diazepam Metabolism

Two initial routes form nordiazepam and temazepam. Both can lead to oxazepam. Conjugation produces inactive glucuronides for urinary excretion.

Pathways, teaching notes & sources

    Phase I oxidation forms active metabolites. Phase II glucuronidation (UGTs) forms inactive conjugates, followed by urinary excretion. Temazepam mainly undergoes direct conjugation.

    *CYP3A4 is supported by human enzyme studies for both steps leading to oxazepam; other CYP contributions have been reported. Enzyme labels are not exhaustive.

    Half-lives describe plasma elimination and do not specify duration of sedation. Values after direct temazepam or oxazepam dosing do not predict their time course when formed from diazepam. Age, hepatic function, genotype and interacting drugs can affect disposition.
